# Synthetic product terms and policy text

Evidence identifier: `banking.evidence.files-inspection`

Synthetic product terms and policy text written for this pack; not an institution document. Each tenant replaces it with its own approved sources.

## Instant-access deposit (synthetic)

The deposit pays an illustrative variable rate published in the synthetic product catalogue. The holder may withdraw at any time. Rates, fees and eligibility are examples only and are set by each tenant.

## Card purchases (synthetic)

Purchases are booked to the card account on the booking day. An illustrative purchase rate applies to balances not repaid in full. Each tenant defines its own limits, rates and dispute handling.

## Data-handling policy (synthetic)

Party, account and transaction records are referenced only by opaque identifiers. No name, address, birth date, national identifier, phone number or e-mail address is held. Every threshold in this pack is illustrative and tenant-overridable.
